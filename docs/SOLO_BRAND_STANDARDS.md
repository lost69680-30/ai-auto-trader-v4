# SOLO — The Hidden Sovereign
## Brand standards · v1.0

### Brand architecture
- **Product name:** SOLO
- **Signature identity:** THE HIDDEN SOVEREIGN
- **Primary emblem:** crowned lion-S monogram. The crown, lion mane and central S are one inseparable mark; do not add unrelated symbols.
- **Primary tagline:** “Trade with Confidence.”
- **Supporting line:** “Intelligence in Every Decision.”

### Emblem construction
The emblem is built from four centered vector paths: crown, crown band, lion-mane silhouette with face opening, and the S monogram. All dark, light, monochrome, launcher and notification variants reuse the same path geometry. Android adaptive icons use the same foreground geometry with an Obsidian background and a monochrome layer.

- Preserve the centered geometry and proportions. Never stretch, skew, rotate, crop, outline twice or add a second logo.
- Keep clear space around the mark at least the height of the crown band.
- Do not place wordmark text inside the launcher or notification icon.
- Use the complete emblem at 48 px and above. The 24 px notification version uses the same geometry in a single color; do not add small decorative details.
- Use the dark mark on Silk Ivory / light surfaces and the Champagne Gold mark on Obsidian / dark surfaces.
- The monochrome Android themed icon uses a single white foreground; the system supplies the surface tint.

Use contrast-adjusted text colors in light mode when the pure palette accent would be difficult to read. Never use color alone to communicate risk or freshness; pair it with a clear label.

### Typography and wordmark
- Render **SOLO** in a bold, clean sans-serif with restrained letter spacing.
- Render **THE HIDDEN SOVEREIGN** as the signature line in small caps or a restrained uppercase treatment.
- Keep the primary tagline secondary to the product name.
- Avoid decorative display fonts, distressed effects, metallic gradients and excessive tracking.

### Application
- Launcher: adaptive icon with Obsidian background, Champagne Gold crowned lion-S foreground, and the matching monochrome layer.
- Notification: same geometry, single-color white vector, no background.
- Dark UI: Obsidian / Graphite surfaces with Champagne Gold accents.
- Light UI: Silk Ivory / white surfaces with Antique Gold or Midnight Bronze for readable emphasis.
- Settings/footer signature: “SOLO · THE HIDDEN SOVEREIGN”.
- Do not imply that research-only agents, strategy experiments or unverified market states are active through decorative badges or branding.

### Safety and trust
Branding must not imply guaranteed profit, live trading, guaranteed uptime or verified AI autonomy. The product remains paper-only and read-only for exchange access; all market and risk states must retain their explicit freshness and verification labels.

### Review status
The vector assets and Android adaptive-icon resources are implemented in the application source and Render assembly. No independent trademark clearance or similarity search has been completed. Complete that review before commercial launch or filing a trademark.
